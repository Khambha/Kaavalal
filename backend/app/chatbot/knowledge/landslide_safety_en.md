# Landslide Safety (Kerala)

## Warning signs
- Heavy or continuous rain for several days, especially in hilly areas of Idukki, Wayanad, Kozhikode, Malappuram, Kottayam and Pathanamthitta.
- Cracks in the ground, roads or walls; tilting trees or poles.
- Muddy water in streams, sudden drop or rise in stream flow.
- Unusual rumbling sound from the hillside.
- Springs or water seeping out of the slope where it was dry before.

## What to do
- If authorities issue a warning or you notice these signs, move out early to a safer, flat area away from slopes and streams. Do not wait to be sure.
- Move sideways, out of the path of the slide, rather than running downhill along it.
- Stay away from river channels and valleys during heavy rain.
- Listen to local announcements and follow evacuation orders from the district administration.
- Take your emergency kit, medicines and documents, but do not delay leaving.

## After a landslide
- Stay away from the slide area; more slides can follow.
- Report trapped people to 112 or 1077 immediately. Do not enter the debris yourself.
- Check for injured people nearby and give first aid if you can do so safely.
- Report broken utility lines to the authorities.
