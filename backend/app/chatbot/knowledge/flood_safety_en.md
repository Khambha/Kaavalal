# Flood Safety (Kerala)

## Before a flood
- Know your nearest relief camp and the safest route to it. Ask your panchayat or ward office.
- Keep an emergency kit ready: drinking water, dry food, torch, power bank, first-aid kit, essential medicines, ID copies in a waterproof bag.
- Move valuables, documents and electrical items to a higher place.
- Follow alerts from KSDMA, IMD and your district collector.

## During a flood
- Move to higher ground early. Do not wait for water to enter the house.
- Switch off the main electricity supply if it is safe to do so. Do not touch electrical equipment while standing in water.
- Never walk, swim or drive through flood water. Six inches of moving water can knock an adult down.
- Avoid bridges and causeways covered by water.
- Do not drink flood water. Use boiled or bottled water.
- Keep children and elderly people close and help them first.

## After a flood
- Return home only when authorities say it is safe.
- Watch for snakes, broken glass and weak walls.
- Do not use electrical appliances that got wet until a qualified electrician checks them.
- Disinfect wells and clean surfaces. Boil drinking water to prevent diarrhoea, leptospirosis and typhoid.
- Rat fever (leptospirosis): if you waded through flood water and develop fever or body pain, see a doctor and mention the exposure.

If you are in danger, call 112 or the district control room 1077.
