# Basic First Aid in Disasters

Call 108 (ambulance) or 112 for serious injuries. These are first steps only, not a replacement for a doctor.

## Bleeding
- Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth. Do not remove the cloth if soaked; add more on top.
- Raise the injured limb if there is no suspected fracture.

## Not breathing / no pulse
- Call 112 or 108. Start chest compressions: push hard and fast in the centre of the chest, about 100 to 120 per minute, until help arrives.

## Drowning rescue
- Do not jump in unless trained. Throw a rope, floating object or pole.
- After the person is out, check breathing. If not breathing, start CPR and call 108.

## Snake bite
- Keep the person calm and still. Keep the bitten limb at heart level.
- Remove rings and tight items. Do not cut the wound, do not suck the venom, do not apply a tight tourniquet.
- Go to the nearest hospital quickly.

## Fractures
- Do not move the person unnecessarily. Support the injured part with a splint or folded cloth.

## Burns
- Cool with clean running water for 10 to 20 minutes. Do not apply ice, toothpaste or oil.

## Hypothermia (cold and wet)
- Move to shelter, remove wet clothes, wrap in dry blankets and give warm drinks if conscious.

## Water-borne illness after floods
- Diarrhoea: drink ORS and boiled water. See a doctor if it lasts more than a day, or there is blood or high fever.
- Fever after wading in flood water may be leptospirosis. See a doctor soon.
