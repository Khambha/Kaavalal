# Lightning, Heavy Rain and Thunderstorm Safety

## Lightning
- Go indoors into a solid building or a vehicle with a metal roof. Do not shelter under isolated trees.
- Stay away from open fields, hilltops, water bodies and metal fences.
- Do not use corded phones or touch plumbing and electrical appliances during a thunderstorm.
- Unplug sensitive electronics before the storm if possible.
- If someone is struck by lightning, call 108. The person carries no charge and is safe to touch. Start CPR if they are not breathing.

## Colour-coded IMD rain alerts
- Green: no action needed.
- Yellow: be aware, keep updated.
- Orange: be prepared; avoid travel in vulnerable areas.
- Red: take action; follow instructions from authorities and evacuate if told.

## Strong winds and sea
- Fishermen should follow IMD and INCOIS warnings and not go to sea when a warning is in force.
- Secure loose objects, and stay away from weak trees, hoardings and electric posts.
