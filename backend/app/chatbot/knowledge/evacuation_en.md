# Evacuation Checklist and Relief Camps

## When told to evacuate
- Leave immediately. Do not go back for belongings.
- Lock the house and switch off electricity and gas if safe.
- Go to the relief camp or safe location announced by your panchayat or district administration.

## What to carry
- Drinking water and dry food for at least a day
- Essential medicines, including regular medicines for diabetes, blood pressure, etc.
- Aadhaar, ration card and other ID copies in a waterproof cover
- Torch, power bank, charged phone
- Warm clothes, a change of clothes, baby supplies if needed
- Cash in small notes

## At the relief camp
- Register your family with the camp officer so rescue teams can account for everyone.
- Report any health problems, pregnancy, disability or elderly members so they get priority care.
- Use only safe drinking water and follow hygiene instructions.
- Keep your phone number registered so relatives can find you.

## Pets and livestock
- If possible, move livestock to higher ground and leave enough water and fodder.
- Ask the camp officer about arrangements for pets.

## Rescue requests
If you or someone else is trapped, call 112 or your district control room 1077, or submit a rescue request in the Kaavalal app. Give your exact location, number of people and anyone who is elderly, sick or a child.
